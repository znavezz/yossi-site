Privacy.

Yossi collects nothing.

Effective 29 October 2026.

What Yossi keeps

Your home: its name, the people in it, tasks, what was done, expenses and payments. Yossi stores this on your devices and in your own iCloud, encrypted. When you invite roommates, iCloud shares that home with them, and only them.

What we see

Nothing. Yossi has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it contains no code from other companies. Your data never reaches the people who make Yossi.

Notifications

Reminders are planned on your device. Alerts about a roommate’s activity come from iCloud noticing a change in your shared home.

iCloud

Your iCloud account and its storage are provided by Apple and covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Removing your data

Household > Leave Home, or Delete Home if you created it, removes the home from your device. Deleting it removes it from iCloud for everyone.

Children

Yossi is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes, this page will say so first. Questions: open an issue.
