Yossi.

A shared home, in order.

Join your home.

Four steps on your iPhone. Once.

  1. Open the invitation.

    An email from TestFlight. Open it on your iPhone and tap View in TestFlight.

  2. Get TestFlight.

    If asked, install Apple’s free TestFlight app. It’s how Yossi arrives before the App Store.

  3. Install Yossi.

    Tap Accept, then Install. Say yes to Automatic Updates, and new versions arrive on their own.

  4. Join the home.

    Open the invite link your roommate sends you. Join, then pick who you are.

You need an iPhone with iOS 18 or later, signed in to iCloud. No account, no password.

Questions.

The short answers.

Something isn’t showing on a roommate’s phone.

Pull down on Today to sync. Both phones need the internet and iCloud. Changes usually arrive within a minute.

I’m not getting reminders.

Check Settings > Notifications > Yossi on your iPhone. In the app, Household > Reminders chooses which tasks remind you.

Can I mute one task?

Open the task and use Notify me. It only changes your phone.

How does rent split work?

Give each person a set amount in the rent task. Pay your part or all of it. Money works out who paid more, month by month.

How do I leave a home?

Household > Leave Home. The home stays for everyone else. Whoever created it can delete it for everyone.

Where is my data?

In your iCloud, shared only with the people in your home. Nobody else sees it, including us. Privacy

Message whoever invited you, or open an issue.
